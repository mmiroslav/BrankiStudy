Your decks, within reach.

01 /

Download and study offline

Sign in to AnkiWeb and download your collection and media. Your downloaded cards and saved reviews stay available without an internet connection.

02 /

Review at your own pace

Reveal the answer, then choose Again, Hard, Good, or Easy. Anki’s scheduling engine records your progress and plans your next review.

03 /

Keep your progress together

Use Sync now before and after studying on another device. You can also study online through AnkiWeb, connect to desktop Anki, or try the sample decks.

Clear about your information

You choose how to connect.

The app has no advertising or third-party tracking SDKs. Downloaded cards live on your device; AnkiWeb sync and online study exchange data with AnkiWeb. Read how local storage, saved sign-ins, and support emails are handled.
