Privacy Policy for BrankiStudy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

BrankiStudy stores downloaded cards and study progress on your iPhone. If you connect to AnkiWeb, the app exchanges account and study data with AnkiWeb to provide the features you select. Desktop mode communicates with your paired computer. We do not operate a BrankiStudy account server, automatically receive your decks, or include advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.

1. Who we are

BrankiStudy is developed by Miroslav Milivojević. In this policy, “we” and “us” refer to the developer. This policy covers the BrankiStudy iPhone app and this support website.

BrankiStudy is an independent app using Anki’s open-source core. Anki and AnkiWeb are projects of Ankitects, whose services have their own privacy practices.

For privacy questions or requests, email miroslav.p.milivojevic@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores information needed to provide the study modes you use, including:

  • Downloaded decks, notes, card content, images, audio, video, and other collection media.
  • Review history, ratings, due dates, scheduling information, daily deck limits, and pending changes awaiting sync.
  • Your AnkiWeb email or username, account-specific local profile information, and sync status.
  • Saved connection information, app appearance, audio and haptic preferences, and sample progress while you use sample decks.
  • Local collection backups created when needed before a full-sync replacement.

This information is used to display your library, render cards, play media, schedule reviews, restore your selected study source, and synchronize your collection. It is stored in the app’s local storage and, for saved credentials, the iOS Keychain.

Downloading a collection creates a local copy of the data in that collection. Card content can contain personal or sensitive information that you or a deck author included. We do not automatically receive that content.

3. Connecting to AnkiWeb

AnkiWeb features require you to choose to connect an AnkiWeb account. BrankiStudy communicates directly with AnkiWeb and its service domains, including ankiweb.net, its sync servers, and ankiuser.net. These connections do not pass through a BrankiStudy server.

Sync key and offline library. Your email and password are sent to AnkiWeb over HTTPS to obtain an account token. The app does not save the password in its credential storage. It saves the issued token and account information so you can verify the connection and sync again. Downloading and syncing exchange your collection, review history, scheduling information, deck settings, and media with AnkiWeb. Reviews made offline remain local until you sync.

Website login and online study. You sign in on AnkiWeb’s own page in the app’s dedicated browser. AnkiWeb provides session cookies, which the app uses for account verification, deck access, online review, media, and deck-limit changes. Online ratings and settings changes are sent to AnkiWeb as you use those features.

Like other online services, AnkiWeb receives connection information such as your IP address and request metadata. AnkiWeb operates its service and determines its own retention and account-deletion practices. See the AnkiWeb privacy policy. We cannot delete an AnkiWeb account or its server-side records on your behalf.

4. Connecting to desktop Anki

If you choose Desktop mode, the app connects to the companion service at the computer address you enter. It sends your pairing token and requests the deck information, current cards, media, review actions, and settings needed for desktop study. Ratings and supported settings changes are recorded by Anki on that computer.

The app saves your desktop address and pairing token in the iOS Keychain. This connection uses your configured desktop service rather than a developer-operated relay. On a physical iPhone, the app requires HTTPS with a trusted certificate.

iOS may ask for Local Network permission to connect to your computer. You can change this permission in iPhone Settings. Desktop Anki and its add-ons may process or sync information separately according to their configuration.

5. Saved sign-ins and security

AnkiWeb sync tokens, desktop pairing credentials, and a backup of the website session cookies are stored in separate iOS Keychain entries configured for access while the device is unlocked and for this device only.

The website login also uses a dedicated persistent WebKit store for cookies and other website data. The app does not import your Safari login or cookies. The sync-key and website sessions are separate, with separate sign-out controls.

Network connections to AnkiWeb and a paired physical desktop use HTTPS. Downloaded collections and local backups rely on iOS app storage and device protection; the app does not add a separate password or end-to-end encryption layer to these files or to AnkiWeb sync.

No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect access to your device and account, and never send passwords, sync tokens, cookies, or pairing tokens to support.

6. Diagnostics, advertising, and tracking

The app does not include advertising, third-party analytics, third-party crash-reporting SDKs, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell or rent your personal information or use it for advertising.

The app displays limited connection diagnostics and writes operational events to the device’s system log. These can include request stages, HTTP status codes, network error codes, response sizes, sync state, and card or pending-review counts. The connection diagnostic summaries are designed to exclude passwords, tokens, cookies, account email addresses, and card content.

BrankiStudy does not automatically upload these diagnostic logs to us. If you choose to share diagnostics, a screenshot, or an operating-system diagnostic report, we receive the information you send. Check it for personal information first.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your downloaded library, media, local collection backups, and saved preferences remain on the iPhone until its app data is removed. Signing out or closing the offline library does not delete downloaded cards or unsynced reviews.

  • AnkiWeb sync sign-in: in the app’s AnkiWeb account screen, select Sync key and use Sign out of AnkiWeb to remove the saved sync credential.
  • Website login: select Website login and use Sign out of website or Clear website session to clear its dedicated website data and saved cookie backup.
  • Desktop pairing: use Disconnect desktop in the app to remove the phone’s saved desktop credential. Disable the desktop companion or rotate its token to revoke access through that pairing.
  • Downloaded study data: use Delete App in iOS to remove the app’s local collection, media, and local collection backups. Offloading the app retains its documents and data.

Keychain credentials can survive deleting an app. Use the relevant sign-out and disconnect controls before deleting BrankiStudy if you want to clear its saved credentials. Signing out locally does not revoke sessions on other devices.

Removing the app does not delete your AnkiWeb account, data already synced to AnkiWeb, your desktop collection, exported files, or existing device backups. Manage those copies separately with the relevant service or device. Restoring a device backup may restore app data.

We do not hold a server copy of your library and cannot recover unsynced local reviews or erase data held only on your device, desktop, or AnkiWeb account.

8. When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or diagnostics you choose to send. We use this information to respond, investigate the issue, and maintain relevant support history.

Support correspondence is handled through Gmail under Google’s privacy policy. We do not add you to a marketing list or use your support messages for advertising.

We keep correspondence only as long as needed to handle your request, maintain relevant support history, or meet applicable legal obligations. You can request access, correction, or deletion by emailing the address above. Share only what is needed to explain your issue; a full collection or private account credentials are not required for an initial support request.

9. This website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising, cookies, tracking pixels, contact forms, or externally hosted fonts to these pages.

GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes when serving GitHub Pages, as described in its GitHub Pages documentation. GitHub’s handling of hosting information is covered by the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

External links and email links open separate services with their own privacy practices. This website’s lack of cookies is separate from the AnkiWeb session cookies used inside the app.

10. Apple services and device backups

Apple handles App Store distribution and may process app usage information, diagnostics, or device backups according to your device settings and its own policies. See Apple’s privacy policy.

iOS or computer-based device backups may include the app’s downloaded study data, depending on your backup settings. These are separate from AnkiWeb sync and from collection backups stored inside the app. We do not operate those device backup services.

If you export a collection or save a backup through another app or service, that location has its own access, retention, and privacy practices.

11. Your choices and privacy requests

You choose whether to connect AnkiWeb, pair a desktop, download a collection, sync changes, or send a support message. Sample decks can be used without an account. Saved offline data remains available after local sign-out, so use device protection and the deletion steps above when sharing or disposing of an iPhone.

For personal information you have sent us, contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or another right available under applicable law. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, request a portable copy, or raise a concern with a data protection authority. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law and may need to verify that a request relates to your information.

For information held by AnkiWeb, Apple, Google, or GitHub, use that provider’s privacy controls and contact details. Their service locations and international data handling are described in their respective policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when our privacy practices change. For questions about this policy, email miroslav.p.milivojevic@gmail.com.
