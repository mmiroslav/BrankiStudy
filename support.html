A few useful answers

Using BrankiStudy

How do I start studying my AnkiWeb decks offline?

On the connection screen, choose AnkiWeb, select Sync key, and sign in with your AnkiWeb email and password. Tap Use offline library to download your collection and begin downloading its media.

Choose a deck, think of the answer, reveal it, and select Again, Hard, Good, or Easy. Reviews save immediately on your iPhone. Downloaded cards work offline; images and audio need to finish downloading before they are available offline too.

If you just want to explore the controls, choose Try with sample decks. Samples do not require an account.

What is the difference between Sync key and Website login?

Sync key connects your downloaded library to AnkiWeb. After the initial download, you can study offline and use Sync now to exchange progress later.

Website login opens AnkiWeb’s own sign-in page. After signing in, tap Study decks on this iPhone for online study. This mode needs an internet connection and saves answers directly to AnkiWeb. Open AnkiWeb decks is also available as a website fallback.

The two sign-ins are separate. Signing out of one does not sign out of the other.

How do I keep progress in sync with my other devices?

In the offline library, use Sync now before and after studying on another device, and sync that device with the same AnkiWeb account. Your local review history and deck-limit changes are exchanged with AnkiWeb during sync.

Collection sync and media download have separate progress. If a connection fails, downloaded cards and saved reviews remain on the iPhone. Reconnect and sync again to finish.

For an additional backup, use Anki on your computer to export your collection with scheduling and media after syncing.

Why am I being asked to keep AnkiWeb or keep iPhone?

Some collection changes require a full sync. Keep AnkiWeb · download replaces this iPhone’s collection with the AnkiWeb copy. Keep iPhone · upload replaces the AnkiWeb collection with the iPhone copy.

Choose the copy containing the work you want to keep. The app creates a local collection backup before replacing an established iPhone collection. This is not an off-device backup and does not make the two choices equivalent.

If you are unsure which copy is current, cancel and contact support before choosing a direction.

Sign-in or verification failed. What should I try?

Check your connection and confirm that you can sign in to AnkiWeb in your browser. AnkiWeb manages its own accounts and password recovery.

For Sync key, use Verify connection to retry a saved token. If the app reports that it has expired or been rejected, sign in again. For Website login, use Verify website connection or clear the website session and sign in again.

If the problem continues, send the error message and the Connection diagnostics or Website connection diagnostics shown in the app. Do not send your credentials.

What should I do if a review is interrupted?

In the offline library, reviews commit locally. Reopen the library to continue, and sync when you have a connection.

During online website study, use Reload current card after a connection interruption or returning from the background. For desktop study, use Reconnect to reviewer. These actions read the current state without automatically sending the last rating again.

Flipping a card only reveals the answer. A review is recorded when you select a rating.

Why are images or audio missing, or a card displayed differently?

For offline study, run Sync now and allow media download to finish. Downloaded images, audio, and video also need to use a format supported by iOS. External media links are unavailable in the offline library.

BrankiStudy presents cards using a native iPhone layout, so imported styling and fonts may look different. Templates relying on JavaScript, add-on filters, interactive typed answers, live MathJax, or scripted image occlusion have compatibility limits.

If a card cannot be displayed safely for grading, the app may disable its ratings. Contact support with a description or a small example that contains no private information.

How do daily limits and Undo work?

Open a deck’s Daily limits. In the offline library, everyday and today-only overrides apply to that deck without editing its shared preset. Review limits can also constrain new cards, and learning cards may remain due after a daily limit is reached.

Website login reads and changes AnkiWeb’s everyday limits. These settings can affect other decks that share the same preset; the screen explains this before saving. Website mode does not offer today-only overrides or Undo.

Offline Undo applies to reviews made in the active local study session. Desktop Undo is available only while the desktop’s current review and undo state still match.

How do I connect to desktop Anki?

Desktop mode requires Anki, AnkiConnect, and the included BrankiStudy companion add-on on your computer. The add-on appears as Anki Study iPhone Companion in Anki. It needs an enabled relay, a private pairing token, and a trusted HTTPS address reachable from your iPhone.

Choose Desktop in BrankiStudy and enter that address and token. Allow Local Network access if iOS asks, and keep Anki open while studying. Keep this service on your private network; do not expose AnkiConnect directly to the internet.

If you need help setting it up, contact support. For AnkiWeb offline or website study, your computer can remain off.

Does signing out delete my cards or reviews?

No. Sign out of AnkiWeb removes the saved sync credential. Your downloaded collection, media, and unsynced reviews remain on the iPhone. Use Open downloaded library to return to them; sign in to the same AnkiWeb account to sync again.

Sign out of website or Clear website session clears the website login stored by the app. Disconnect desktop removes its saved pairing token. Close offline library simply closes the library.

None of these actions deletes your AnkiWeb account or the collection on your computer.

How do I remove my data?

If you want to keep your progress, complete a sync first. Sign out of AnkiWeb, clear the website session, and disconnect desktop pairing if you used those connections. To remove the downloaded library, media, and local collection backups, use Delete App in iOS. Offloading the app keeps its documents and data.

iOS Keychain credentials may survive app deletion, so use the app’s sign-out controls before removing it. Copies in AnkiWeb, desktop Anki, device backups, and exported files must be managed separately.

Read the retention and deletion section for details, or email us about information you previously sent to support.

Is BrankiStudy the official Anki iPhone app?

BrankiStudy is an independent app that uses Anki’s open-source core. Anki and AnkiWeb are projects of Ankitects. BrankiStudy is not the official AnkiMobile app.

Contact BrankiStudy support for problems in this app. AnkiWeb account access, account deletion, and service policies are managed by AnkiWeb.
